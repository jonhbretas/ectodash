Purpose: turn the proven schema and the eligibility classifier into the actual daily reminder mechanism, correctly idempotent under Vercel's documented at-least-once cron delivery.
Output: a working `GET /api/cron/reminders` Route Handler, deployable and locally testable, that sends one deduplicated email per (demanda, responsável, tipo, day) and leaves a complete run-log trail — not yet wired into Vercel Production (that is plan 07-03's env-var checkpoint).
